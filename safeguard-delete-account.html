Delete Your SafeGuard Account

Last updated: July 2026

You can delete your SafeGuard Family account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what data is affected.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From the SafeGuard Parent App
  1. Open the SafeGuard Parent app
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm by entering your PIN or password
Option 2: By Email
  1. Send an email to info@shenbee.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address associated with your account
  4. We will process your request within 7 business days

What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your user profile and authentication credentials
  • Family group and device registrations
  • App rules, URL rules, and screen time configurations
  • Location history and geo-fence settings
  • Activity logs and usage reports
  • SOS event history
  • Subscription metadata (billing is managed by Google Play)

What Data Is Retained

  • Google Play purchase records — managed by Google, not SafeGuard. To manage subscriptions, go to Google Play > Subscriptions.
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics — cannot be linked back to your account.

Deletion Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Data is removed immediately from live systems. Backups are purged within 30 days.
  • Email request: Processed within 7 business days. Data removed from backups within 30 days.

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is irreversible. You cannot recover your data after deletion.
  • Deleting the parent account removes the entire family group, including all linked child devices.
  • Active subscriptions are not automatically cancelled. Please cancel your subscription in Google Play before deleting your account to avoid further charges.
  • Local Edition data stored on-device is not affected by account deletion. To remove local data, uninstall the app.
