Privacy Policy — 易问 (Augur)

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 易问 (Augur) mobile application ("the App"), published by Shenbee ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Using the App offline (no account)

The core of the App — casting a hexagram through the coin ritual and reading the selected classical text (原文) — works fully offline and without an account. If you only use the App this way, we do not collect any personal information; your casting history is stored locally on your device.

2. Signing in with Google

To use the online interpretation features, you may sign in with Google. When you do, we receive from Google your email address, display name, and a Google account identifier, which we use to create your account and manage your subscription status. We do not receive your Google password. Conversation history remains local to the device.

3. Content you create and how it is processed

The App stores your divination records — the cast hexagram, the selected classical text, questions, answers, and any attached images — in encrypted local storage on your device. Images are saved as separately encrypted local files and are used for the current reading and later follow-up questions. They are not included as image files in history exports.

When you request an online interpretation, the question, selected classical context, and any images you actively attach are sent through our backend to DeepSeek to produce the answer. The backend does not persist image files in Firestore, object storage, or its own disk, and we do not send your name or email to the interpretation provider. We do not make promises about DeepSeek's own retention practices; consult its current policies for that information.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

The App offers an auto-renewing subscription that unlocks deeper, multi-turn interpretation and a stronger model. Purchases are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, Inc., our purchase-infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives your app-user identifier and the purchase/transaction details from the store to validate and restore your subscription. We never receive or store your credit-card or payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and consent

Users without an active subscription may see ads provided by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, the App asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. Where the platform requires it, Settings includes a Privacy options entry so you can change those choices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Deleting a history item removes its local encrypted image files and conversation record.

To delete your account, use Me → Settings → Delete account. That opens a pre-filled email to maliangoneone@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account — Augur". You can also send that email yourself from (or including) your sign-in email. We handle requests within 30 days. After we process the request we delete the Firebase sign-in account, profile, usage records/counters, and request logs. History is stored locally on the device and is cleared by the in-app local wipe or by uninstalling the App.

An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled by account deletion; manage or cancel it in Google Play. Some data may be kept only where the law requires it (for example purchase, tax, or fraud-prevention records held by Google and RevenueCat).

Step-by-step instructions: Delete Account.

7. Data security

Data in transit is protected with TLS encryption. Chat images are encrypted locally with a key protected by Android Keystore; the backend does not retain those image bytes after processing.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions? Email us at info@shenbee.com. To delete your account or request deletion of your data, use Settings → Delete account or email maliangoneone@gmail.com as described above.
