Privacy Policy — Commute Alarm

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Developer: Shenbee · Contact: info@shenbee.com

1. Summary

Commute Alarm / 到站提醒 works on your device. We do not run accounts or application servers for it. We do not show ads. Location is processed on-device only; we do not collect your location on our servers.

2. Data stored on your device

Favourites (name, coordinates, category), trip settings, optional diagnostic logs, weekday reminder time, language preference, and downloaded region packs. They stay on this device and are removed when you use Settings → Clear app data or uninstall.

3. Location

When you start a trip, the operating system provides location to an Android location foreground service or iOS Core Location so the app can estimate distance to coordinates you saved. We do not upload tracks. This version has no advertising or analytics SDK, so location is never forwarded to ads or analytics.

4. Advertising and purchases

v1 has no ads, no User Messaging Platform, and no in-app purchases. We do not use an advertising identifier.

5. Permissions

Precise location (and background location only while a trip you started is running), notifications, optional physical activity, and on Android a visible foreground-service notification. Internet is used only if you choose to download a region pack.

6. Children

Commute Alarm is not directed to children under 13.

7. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear app data, or uninstall the app. There is no cloud copy. If you email us, we only hold the contents of that email.

8. Map data

Offline packs are derived from OpenStreetMap. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

10. Contact

info@shenbee.com · shenbee.com

中文

隐私政策 — 到站提醒

最后更新：2026年10月20日 · 开发者：Shenbee · 联系：info@shenbee.com

1. 概述

到站提醒在你的设备上运行，没有账号和应用服务器，没有广告。位置仅在本机处理，我们不会在自己的服务器上收集你的位置。

2. 本机数据

收藏（名称、坐标、分类）、行程设置、可选诊断日志、工作日提醒时间、语言偏好、以及下载的地区包。仅保存在本机，可在「设置 → 清除本机数据」或卸载时删除。

3. 位置

你主动开始行程后，系统定位会提供给 Android 前台服务或 iOS Core Location，用于估算与已保存坐标的距离。我们不上传轨迹。此版本没有广告或分析 SDK，位置也不会转发给广告/分析。

4. 广告与购买

v1 没有广告、没有 UMP、没有应用内购买，也不使用广告标识符。

5. 权限

精确位置（仅在你开启的行程中需要后台位置）、通知、可选的运动权限。Android 行程中会显示可见的前台服务通知。仅在你选择下载地区包时使用网络。

6. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

7. 删除数据

使用「设置 → 清除本机数据」或卸载。没有云端副本。

8. 地图数据

离线地区包来自 OpenStreetMap。Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL。

9. 变更

我们可能更新本政策，以「最后更新」日期为准。

10. 联系我们

info@shenbee.com
