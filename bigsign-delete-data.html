Data Deletion — BigSign (大字举牌)

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Developer: Shenbee · Contact: info@shenbee.com

This page explains how to delete data associated with BigSign (大字举牌), published by Shenbee. The app does not require an account. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Recent signs, saved signs, your current draft and language setting are stored locally on your device only.

  1. Open Settings → Clear all signs, which deletes recents, saved signs and the current draft (language and any remove-ads purchase stay).
  2. Or uninstall the app, which permanently erases all local app data.

2. Delete purchase-related data

If you bought “Remove ads”, a randomly generated app-user identifier and the purchase record are held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, Inc. (used only to validate and restore purchases). To request deletion of that record:

  1. Email info@shenbee.com with the subject line Delete my data — BigSign.
  2. If available, include your Google Play order number so we can locate the record.
  3. We will delete the associated app-user record within 30 days and confirm by email.

3. What is deleted vs. retained

Deleted: on-device signs (via Clear all signs or uninstall) and the RevenueCat app-user record tied to your request. Retained: transaction records that Google Play and RevenueCat must keep for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, and your language setting if you only use Clear all signs.

4. Contact

Questions about data deletion? Email info@shenbee.com.

数据删除 — 大字举牌（BigSign）

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 9 日 · 开发者：Shenbee · 联系：info@shenbee.com

本页说明如何删除与 大字举牌（BigSign） 相关的数据。本应用无需账号。完整说明见隐私政策。

1. 删除保存在本机的数据

最近举牌、收藏、当前草稿和语言设置都只保存在你的设备上。

  1. 打开「设置 → 清除所有举牌记录」，将删除最近、收藏和当前草稿（语言设置和已购买的去广告会保留）。
  2. 或者卸载应用，将永久清除全部本地应用数据。

2. 删除购买相关数据

如果你购买了「去除广告」，Google Play 与 RevenueCat, Inc. 会保存一个随机应用用户 ID 和购买记录（仅用于验证和恢复购买）。如需删除该记录：

  1. 发送邮件至 info@shenbee.com，主题为 Delete my data — BigSign。
  2. 如可能，请附上 Google Play 订单号以便查找记录。
  3. 我们将在 30 天内删除对应的应用用户记录，并邮件确认。

3. 删除与保留

删除：本机举牌记录（通过清除或卸载）以及与请求对应的 RevenueCat 应用用户记录。保留：Google Play 与 RevenueCat 依法、报税或防作弊需要保留的交易记录；若只使用「清除所有举牌记录」，语言设置会保留。

4. 联系

数据删除相关问题请发邮件至 info@shenbee.com。
