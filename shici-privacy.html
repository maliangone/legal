Privacy Policy — 读诗 (Chinese Poetry)

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 读诗 (Chinese Poetry) mobile application ("the App"), published by Shenbee ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. No account, no sign-in

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any name, email address, or phone number. You can use the App without identifying yourself.

2. Support requests you choose to send

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address you use, the contents of your message, and any device model or operating-system version that you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to and troubleshoot your request; we do not use it for advertising or sell it. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain necessary records, then delete or anonymize it. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing info@shenbee.com.

3. Information stored on your device

Your preferences and activity — favorites, playlists, and playback settings — are stored locally on your device only (via the operating system's local storage). We do not collect, transmit, or have access to this information. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Whether you have unlocked the full catalog is also stored locally. The authoritative purchase record for that unlock is held by the App Store you purchased through and by RevenueCat, as described in the Purchases section below.

4. Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock all poems. Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, Inc., our purchase-infrastructure provider. To validate and restore your purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app-user identifier and the purchase/transaction details provided by the store. We never receive or store your credit-card or payment details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and the applicable Apple Privacy Policy or Google Privacy Policy.

5. Photos you choose to save

When you choose to save a poem illustration, the App asks only for permission to add that image to your device's photo library. The App does not read your existing photo library, collect your photos, or upload saved images to our servers.

6. No advertising

The App contains no advertising and does not use the device advertising identifier.

7. Children

The App is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Data security & retention

Except for support correspondence described above, preferences stay on your device and we operate no user accounts, so we do not hold those preferences on our servers. Purchase records held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat are retained according to their respective policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions? Email us at info@shenbee.com.
