Xiaoxiao Paint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Xiaoxiao Paint (小小Paint), published by Shenbee, handles information. The App is a local-first drawing tool. It does not require an account.

1. Drawings on your device

Sketches, stickers, text, album pages, and thumbnails stay in the App's sandbox on your device. We do not upload your artwork to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes that sandbox data. Exported files in your system photo library remain under your control.

2. Photo library (save only)

When you choose to export or save a drawing, the App may ask for permission to add that image to your photo library. The App does not import photos from your library or camera, and it does not upload saved images to our servers.

3. Advertising

If you have not purchased the optional supporter item, the App shows a non-personalized banner ad from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Ads are requested with a non-personalized flag. The App does not present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use data from the App to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Google and its advertising partners may process technical information such as IP address (used to estimate coarse location), device and advertising identifiers where the platform allows, consent signals, ad impressions and taps, advertising data, crash logs, and performance diagnostics. Google may use this information for third-party advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, according to Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policy. Where required, the App shows Google's consent form before requesting ads.

The optional one-time supporter purchase removes the banner ad.

4. Optional purchases

The supporter purchase is a one-time, non-consumable item processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and validated through RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app-user identifier and the purchase, transaction, and entitlement details provided by the store. We do not receive your payment-card details or artwork. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy, Apple's Privacy Policy, and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing, ratings, and support

System share sheets and store rating prompts open only when you choose those actions. If you email us, we receive the address and message you send, and use them only to respond. We retain support mail only as reasonably necessary.

6. What we do not do

We do not require an account, sell personal information, or use the App for cross-app tracking. Drawings are not used for advertising.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.

8. Contact

Questions? Email info@shenbee.com.

小小Paint — 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 10 月 2 日

本政策说明 Shenbee 发布的 小小Paint（Xiaoxiao Paint） 如何处理信息。这是一款本地优先的画板，不需要注册账号。

1. 设备上的作品

画作、贴纸、文字、相册页和缩略图保存在应用沙盒中，不会上传到开发者服务器。卸载应用后， 沙盒数据会被删除。你导出到系统相册的文件由你自行管理。

2. 相册权限（仅保存）

只有在你选择导出或保存作品时，应用才会请求把图片写入相册。应用不会从相册或相机导入照片， 也不会把已保存的图片上传到我们的服务器。

3. 广告

若未购买可选的支持项目，应用会显示由 Google AdMob 提供的 非个性化横幅广告。广告请求带有非个性化标记。应用不会弹出苹果的跟踪许可 （App Tracking Transparency），也不会把应用内数据用于跨应用或跨网站跟踪。

Google 及其广告合作伙伴可能处理 IP 地址（用于估算粗略位置）、设备/广告标识符（在系统允许时）、 同意信号、广告展示与点击、广告数据、崩溃日志和性能诊断，用于第三方广告、分析和防欺诈。详见 Google 隐私政策。在需要时，应用会先显示 Google 同意表单再请求广告。

一次性支持购买后，横幅广告会被移除。

4. 可选购买

支持项目是一次性、非消耗型内购，由 App Store 或 Google Play 处理，并由 RevenueCat 校验。RevenueCat 可能收到匿名用户标识以及商店提供的购买与权益信息。 我们不会收到银行卡信息或你的画作。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

5. 分享、评分与客服

系统分享和商店评分只在你主动操作时打开。若你发邮件，我们会收到你留下的地址和内容，仅用于回复。

6. 我们不会做的事

不要求账号，不出售个人信息，不做跨应用跟踪。画作不会用于广告。

7. 联系

隐私问题请联系 info@shenbee.com。
