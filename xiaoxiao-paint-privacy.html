Xiaoxiao Paint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains how Xiaoxiao Paint (小小Paint), published by Shenbee, handles information.

1. Local drawings and photos

Drawings, imported photos, thumbnails, and custom stickers are stored in the App's local storage on your device. The App does not upload your photos or artwork to our servers.

The camera and photo-library permissions are used only when you choose to take, import, or save an image. Exported files in your system photo library are managed by you.

2. Optional purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to support development. Google Play or the Apple App Store processes payment. RevenueCat helps validate and restore purchase status and may receive purchase and app-user identifiers. We do not receive your payment-card details or artwork through this service.

3. On-device processing and system services

Selfie segmentation for custom stickers is performed on the device on images you select. System sharing and app-review prompts open only when you choose those actions. The App contains no advertising, does not require an account, and does not sell personal information or use cross-app tracking.

4. Support requests

If you email us, we receive the email address and message you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as reasonably necessary.

5. Deletion and changes

Deleting a drawing or uninstalling the App removes the App's local data; exported files in your system photo library remain under your control. We may update this policy by changing the date above.

6. Contact

Questions? Email info@shenbee.com.
