Data Deletion — 易问 (Augur)

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with the 易问 (Augur) app, published by Shenbee. Account deletion is requested by email; the current steps are on Delete Account. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we store.

1. Delete on-device data

If you used the App offline without signing in, your casting history is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the App permanently erases it. If you are signed in, Settings → Delete account also offers Clear local data and sign out on this device after you send the deletion email.

2. Delete your account and cloud data

In the App, go to Me → Settings → Delete account. That opens a pre-filled email to maliangoneone@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account — Augur. You can also send that email yourself from (or including) your sign-in email. We'll handle the request within 30 days. After we process it we delete the Firebase sign-in account, profile, usage records/counters, and request logs. See Delete Account for the full steps.

3. Delete purchase-related data

If you subscribed, a randomly generated app-user identifier and the purchase/transaction record are held by Google Play and by our purchase provider RevenueCat, Inc. (used only to validate and restore subscriptions — never for advertising). Request deletion of that record in the same email; include your Google Play order number if available so we can locate it. Manage or cancel billing in Google Play (Subscriptions).

4. What is deleted vs. retained

Deleted: your Firebase sign-in account, profile, usage records/counters, and request logs; on-device history is removed by the in-app local wipe or by uninstalling. Retained: some data may be kept only where the law requires it, including transaction records that Google Play and RevenueCat must keep for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, in line with their own retention policies.

5. Contact

Questions about data deletion? Email maliangoneone@gmail.com.
