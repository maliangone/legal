Delete Account — 易问 (Augur)

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page explains how to delete an account and associated data for the 易问 (Augur) app, published by Shenbee. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we store.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

If the App is installed on your device:

  1. Open 易问 (Augur).
  2. Go to Me → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete.

This deletes your Firebase sign-in account, the users/{uid} cloud record, usage counters, and request logs. Local data on the device is wiped: encrypted divination history, birth profiles, chat images, encryption keys, preferences, and caches. You are then signed out of Google and the purchase SDK.

Deleting the account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel billing in Google Play (Subscriptions) if you no longer want to be charged.

2. Email fallback

If in-app deletion doesn't work, email maliangoneone@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account — Augur from your sign-in email. We'll handle it within 30 days.

3. What is deleted vs. retained

Deleted by the in-app action (or by the email request): your Firebase sign-in account; the users/{uid} cloud record, usage counters, and request logs; local encrypted history, birth profiles, images, keys, and caches.

Retained: Google Play and RevenueCat purchase / tax / fraud-prevention records, according to their policies. Webhook idempotency records are not keyed to your account and expire on their own schedule. Uninstalling the App without deleting the account removes local data but leaves the cloud account until you use Settings → Delete account or the email fallback.

4. Contact

Questions about account deletion? Email maliangoneone@gmail.com.
