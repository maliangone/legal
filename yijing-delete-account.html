Delete Account — 易问 (Augur)

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of an account and associated data for the 易问 (Augur) app, published by Shenbee. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we store.

1. How to request deletion

Account deletion is handled by email, within 30 days of your request.

If the App is installed on your device:

  1. Open 易问 (Augur).
  2. Go to Me → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. The App opens a pre-filled email to maliangoneone@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account — Augur.

You can also email maliangoneone@gmail.com yourself with the subject Delete my account — Augur from (or including) your sign-in email.

We'll handle the request within 30 days.

2. What is deleted

After we process the request we delete: your Firebase sign-in account; profile; usage records and counters; and request logs.

Divination history is stored locally on your device. Clear it with the in-app option Clear local data and sign out on this device after sending the email, or by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling removes local data only; the cloud account remains until we process the email request.

Deleting the account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel billing in Google Play (Subscriptions) if you no longer want to be charged.

3. What may be retained

Some data may be kept only where the law requires it — for example Google Play and RevenueCat purchase, tax, or fraud-prevention records, according to their policies. Webhook idempotency records are not keyed to your account and expire on their own schedule.

4. Contact

Questions about account deletion? Email maliangoneone@gmail.com.
