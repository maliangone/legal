Data Deletion — Commute Alarm

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Developer: Shenbee · Contact: info@shenbee.com

This page explains how to delete data associated with Commute Alarm / 到站提醒, published by Shenbee. The app has no account. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

1. Delete data stored on your device

Favourites, trip settings, diagnostic logs, reminder time, language and region packs are stored only on your device. Location is processed on the device and never uploaded.

  1. Open Settings → Clear app data, or
  2. Uninstall the app (or clear the app's storage in system settings), which permanently erases all local app data.

2. Reset your advertising ID

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device advertising ID. Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Reset or Delete advertising ID. iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can change ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.

3. Delete purchase-related data

If you bought “Remove ads”, an anonymous app-user ID and the purchase record are held by Google Play / Apple and by RevenueCat, Inc. (used only to validate and restore purchases). To request deletion:

  1. Email info@shenbee.com with the subject line Delete my data — Commute Alarm.
  2. If available, include your Google Play or Apple order number.
  3. We will delete the associated RevenueCat app-user record within 30 days and confirm by email.

4. What is deleted vs. retained

Deleted: on-device data (via Clear app data or uninstall) and the RevenueCat app-user record tied to your request. Retained: transaction records that Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention purposes. A deleted purchase record may no longer be restorable; reinstalling and using Restore purchases will create a new record.

中文

数据删除 — 到站提醒

最后更新：2026年10月21日 · 开发者：Shenbee · 联系：info@shenbee.com

本页说明如何删除与到站提醒相关的数据。本应用没有账号。完整说明见隐私政策。

1. 删除保存在本机的数据

收藏、行程设置、诊断日志、提醒时间、语言和地区包都只保存在你的设备上。位置仅在本机处理，绝不上传。

  1. 打开「设置 → 清除本机数据」，或
  2. 卸载应用（或在系统设置中清除应用存储），将永久清除全部本地数据。

2. 重置广告 ID

广告由 Google AdMob 提供，会使用设备广告 ID。Android：「设置 → Google → 广告」（或「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」）→ 重置或删除广告 ID。iOS：「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」。你也可以在应用「设置 → 广告隐私选项」中更改广告同意。

3. 删除购买相关数据

如果你购买了「去除广告」，Google Play / Apple 与 RevenueCat, Inc. 会保存一个匿名应用用户 ID 和购买记录（仅用于验证和恢复购买）。如需删除：

  1. 发送邮件至 info@shenbee.com，主题为 Delete my data — Commute Alarm。
  2. 如可能，请附上 Google Play 或 Apple 订单号。
  3. 我们将在 30 天内删除对应的 RevenueCat 应用用户记录，并邮件确认。

4. 删除与保留

删除：本机数据（通过清除或卸载）以及与请求对应的 RevenueCat 应用用户记录。保留：Google Play、Apple 与 RevenueCat 依法、报税或防作弊需要保留的交易记录。

5. 联系

info@shenbee.com
